Free diagnostic laboratory roster tool

Draft your laboratory roster in minutes. Rules-based, competency-aware, and always a draft for your review.

Describe your laboratory once in an Excel workbook: staffing levels, competencies, shift patterns and bench coverage. Lab Shift Roster generates a draft roster in minutes, then shows you every issue it found.

Your workforce data stays on your device. Lab Shift Roster processes your workbook locally in your browser and does not upload it to Optymum SS. No roster is stored on an external database, and the workbook you download is created on your own computer. Limited information about how the application is used may be sent to Google Analytics; staff, roster, competency, leave and workbook data are never included.

Preparing Lab Shift Roster…

Your workbook needs correcting

    Correct these in your workbook, save it, then upload it again. Everything found is listed together so you can fix it in one pass.

    Roster summary

    This is a draft for you to review. Lab Shift Roster supports your decisions about staffing; it does not make them.

    View issues

    The staff rota is a simplified workbook for circulating to the team: who is working, when, and in which section. It deliberately leaves out competency records, individual hours, fairness analysis, workforce vulnerabilities and the reason for anybody's absence. The manager report keeps the full analysis.

    Issues

    Grouped by how much attention each one needs. Related warnings with the same underlying cause are combined into one item.

    Adjust an assignment

    Change who is working a shift. Lab Shift Roster re-runs every check afterwards, so you see straight away whether the change creates a new problem. Manual changes are kept and are never overwritten when the roster is rebuilt.

    Roster preview

    Scroll sideways to see the whole period. Filter to focus on one shift, section or person.

    Section coverage

    Who was allocated to each laboratory section. One person covers one section at a time, so this reflects who is actually there.

    Contracted hours

    Target hours are contracted weekly hours scaled to the length of the roster period.

    Workforce resilience

    How many people could cover each discipline if somebody were unavailable. This is about the department as a whole, which is a different question from whether one shift is thinly covered.

    How Lab Shift Roster protects your data

    What happens to your data

    Your workbook is read inside your browser tab. It is not uploaded to Optymum SS, and Lab Shift Roster does not store roster information on an external database. The exported workbook is generated on your own device. Once this page has loaded you can disconnect from the network and it will still work.

    We cannot make absolute security guarantees about your own device or network. Please include only the workforce information the tool needs. Do not add sensitive personal details, and never add patient-identifiable information — Lab Shift Roster does not require any.

    Usage analytics

    Limited information about how this application is used may be sent to Google Analytics: page views, and three events recording that a draft roster was generated, a staff rota was exported or a manager report was exported. Google Analytics also records the ordinary technical details any website receives, such as approximate location, device type and browser.

    The events are names alone. No staff, roster, competency, leave, hours, organisation or workbook information is included — not staff names or identifiers, not your file name, not any figure taken from your workbook, and no error text. Nothing about your workforce leaves your device. Advertising features, Google Signals and ad personalisation are switched off.

    How Lab Shift Roster works

    The scheduling engine is rules-based, not artificial intelligence. It fills the shifts you define, in an order that satisfies the hardest requirements first, and it will not break a rule you have set: where it cannot fill a shift without doing so, it leaves the shift short and tells you.

    The application runs a Python runtime compiled to WebAssembly (Pyodide), together with the openpyxl spreadsheet library, entirely inside your browser. This is what allows the tool to be served as static files while keeping your data local.

    About Lab Shift Roster

    Lab Shift Roster is built for diagnostic laboratory managers. It answers one question: do I have the right people, with the right competencies, in the right laboratory areas, at the right time?

    Every roster it produces is a draft for managerial review. It does not replace local workforce policies, HR systems or professional judgement, and the rest interval and other limits are rules you configure for your own laboratory rather than a determination of legal or regulatory compliance.

    The scheduling engine is rules-based. It is not artificial intelligence, and it does not make staffing decisions — the laboratory manager remains responsible for approving every roster.
