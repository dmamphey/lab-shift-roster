Lab Shift Roster

Builds a colour-coded shift rota as an Excel workbook, with a per-person summary and a rule check.

Your data stays on this device. Python runs inside your browser, so the workbook you choose is never uploaded and no rota is sent anywhere. You can disconnect from the network once the page has loaded.

Step 1 · Input workbook

Download the template, replace the example staff with your own, set the date range on the Settings sheet, then add any leave.

Step 2 · Build the rota

Leave the dates blank to use the range on the Settings sheet. The same seed always produces the same rota; change it to reshuffle one you don't like.

What is loading?

The page downloads a Python runtime compiled to WebAssembly (Pyodide, roughly 10–25 MB) plus the openpyxl spreadsheet library, then the roster script itself. It is cached afterwards, so later visits are quick. Nothing you enter is transmitted.

Rules enforced: no shift while on leave · one shift per day · no shift the day after a night · a cap on consecutive days · nights in blocks with rest days after. Shifts and weekends are spread as evenly as possible, with a senior on every shift that needs one. If cover cannot be found without breaking a rule, the shift is left short and reported rather than the rule being broken.
