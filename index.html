LabRoster

A secure and intelligent workforce planning tool for diagnostic laboratories.

Stop wasting hours on lab scheduling. Set up your laboratory data once to generate draft rosters in minutes. Simply input your staffing levels, competencies, shift patterns, and bench coverage to automate your scheduling forever.

Your workforce data stays on your device. LabRoster processes your workbook locally in your browser and does not upload it to Optymum SS. No roster is stored on an external database, and the workbook you download is created on your own computer.

Preparing LabRoster…

Your workbook needs correcting

    Correct these in your workbook, save it, then upload it again. Everything found is listed together so you can fix it in one pass.

    Roster summary

    This is a draft for you to review. LabRoster supports your decisions about staffing; it does not make them.

    View issues

    The staff rota is a simplified workbook for circulating to the team: who is working, when, and in which section. It deliberately leaves out competency records, individual hours, fairness analysis, workforce vulnerabilities and the reason for anybody's absence. The manager report keeps the full analysis.

    Issues

    Grouped by how much attention each one needs. Related warnings with the same underlying cause are combined into one item.

    Adjust an assignment

    Change who is working a shift. LabRoster re-runs every check afterwards, so you see straight away whether the change creates a new problem. Manual changes are kept and are never overwritten when the roster is rebuilt.

    Roster preview

    Scroll sideways to see the whole period. Filter to focus on one shift, section or person.

    Section coverage

    Who was allocated to each laboratory section. One person covers one section at a time, so this reflects who is actually there.

    Contracted hours

    Target hours are contracted weekly hours scaled to the length of the roster period.

    Workforce resilience

    How many people could cover each discipline if somebody were unavailable. This is about the department as a whole, which is a different question from whether one shift is thinly covered.

    LabRoster by Optymum SS generates draft workforce plans for managerial review. It does not replace local workforce policies, HR systems or professional judgement. Rest intervals and other limits are rules you configure for your own laboratory, not a determination of legal or regulatory compliance.

    How LabRoster protects your data

    What happens to your data

    Your workbook is read inside your browser tab. It is not uploaded to Optymum SS, and LabRoster does not store roster information on an external database. The exported workbook is generated on your own device. Once this page has loaded you can disconnect from the network and it will still work.

    We cannot make absolute security guarantees about your own device or network. Please include only the workforce information the tool needs. Do not add sensitive personal details, and never add patient-identifiable information — LabRoster does not require any.

    How LabRoster works

    The scheduling engine is rules-based, not artificial intelligence. It fills the shifts you define, in an order that satisfies the hardest requirements first, and it will not break a rule you have set: where it cannot fill a shift without doing so, it leaves the shift short and tells you.

    The application runs a Python runtime compiled to WebAssembly (Pyodide), together with the openpyxl spreadsheet library, entirely inside your browser. This is what allows the tool to be served as static files while keeping your data local.

    About LabRoster

    LabRoster is developed by Optymum SS for diagnostic laboratory managers. It answers one question: do I have the right people, with the right competencies, in the right laboratory areas, at the right time?

    The scheduling engine is rules-based. It is not artificial intelligence, and it does not make staffing decisions — the laboratory manager remains responsible for approving every roster.
